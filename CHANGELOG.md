# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - First release

- **The shared code of the guild addons** (Guild Professions & Recipes, Bot'tar Sync, Guild Bank Alts): hidden addon messages, the guild roster, a saved database of the members' records and the sync that keeps every member's copy the same.
- Each addon keeps its own prefix, protocol and saved data.
- **Translated into every client language**: English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Korean, Portuguese, Russian and Chinese.
