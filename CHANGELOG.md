# Changelog

## 0.2.0

- **More for the addons built on it**: message types of their own, shorter messages for some changes, changes announced within seconds, test messages, and a saved form of their own for addons that saved their records another way before.
- **Members on an older protocol** are heard only for what they announce: nobody answers their requests in a protocol they could not read.
- **The roster** also tells how many members are online.

## 0.1.0 - First release

- **Shared code for guild addons**: hidden addon messages, the guild roster, a saved database of the members' records and the sync that keeps every member's copy the same.
- Each addon keeps its own prefix, protocol and saved data.
- **Translated into every client language**: English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, Korean, Portuguese, Russian and Chinese.
